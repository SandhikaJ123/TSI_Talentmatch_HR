📄 Step 1: Parsing (Extracting Information)

1 What is Parsing?

Parsing means reading a document (PDF, DOCX, TXT) and extracting the text content so we can analyze it.

How We Parse Documents:

File Type Method What We Extract
PDF pdf-parse library All text content, preserving structure
DOCX mammoth library Text with basic formatting
TXT Direct read Plain text as-is

📝 Example: Parsing a Resume

Input: resume.pdf

Output:

What We Extract from Job Descriptions:

  • Required Skills: JavaScript, React, Node.js, AWS

2 What is Validation?

Validation ensures the extracted data is complete, accurate, and usable for matching.

What We Validate:

🎯 Step 3: Scoring (Calculating Match Quality)

3 What is Scoring?

Scoring calculates how well a resume matches the job requirements. We use a weighted formula that considers multiple factors.

Scoring Methods Available:

🔹 Hybrid Mode (Recommended)

Combines rule-based scoring with AI semantic understanding

  • 75% Rule-based (skills, experience, education)
  • 25% Semantic similarity (AI embeddings)
  • Fast, consistent, and cost-effective
🔹 NLP Mode (Fastest)

Pure rule-based matching using natural language processing

  • 100% Rule-based
  • No API required, fully offline
  • Instant results
🔹 AI Mode (Most Detailed)

Full GPT-4o analysis for detailed insights

  • 100% AI-powered
  • Detailed explanations
  • Slower and more expensive

The Scoring Formula:

Final Score = (Skills × 40%) + (Experience × 25%) + (Education × 20%) + (Overall × 15%)

Default Weights (Customizable):

  • Skills: 40% - Most important factor
  • Experience: 25% - Years of relevant work
  • Education: 20% - Degree level
  • Overall Match: 15% - Keyword density & semantic fit

How Each Component is Scored:

1️⃣ Skills Score (0-100)

Skills Score = (Matched Skills / Required Skills) × 100

Example:

  • Job requires: JavaScript, React, Node.js, AWS, Docker (5 skills)
  • Resume has: JavaScript, React, Node.js, Python (4 skills, 3 match)
  • Score: (3 / 5) × 100 = 60%

Bonus Features:

  • ✅ Synonym detection (React = ReactJS = React.js)
  • ✅ Skill frequency bonus (mentioned multiple times)
  • ✅ Certification bonus (+10 points for AWS Certified, etc.)

2️⃣ Experience Score (0-100)

Experience Score = min(100, (Candidate Years / Required Years) × 100)

Example:

  • Job requires: 5 years
  • Candidate has: 7 years
  • Score: (7 / 5) × 100 = 100% (capped at 100)

How We Detect Experience:

  • "5+ years of experience"
  • "Senior Developer (2018-2023)" → 5 years
  • "Minimum 3 years required"

3️⃣ Education Score (0-100)

Education Level Score
PhD / Doctorate100
Master's Degree85
Bachelor's Degree70
Associate Degree50

Not Detected0

4️⃣ Overall Match Score (0-100)

Uses TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) to measure keyword relevance:

  • Extracts important terms from job description
  • Checks how many appear in the resume
  • Weights terms by importance (rare terms score higher)

In Hybrid Mode: Also includes semantic similarity using AI embeddings (25% weight)

📊 Complete Scoring Example

Job Requirements:

  • Skills: JavaScript, React, Node.js, AWS, Docker
  • Experience: 5 years
  • Education: Bachelor's degree

Candidate Resume:

  • Skills: JavaScript, React, Node.js, Python (3/5 match)
  • Experience: 6 years

  • Skills: 60% (3 out of 5 matched)
  • Experience: 100% (6 years ≥ 5 years required)
  • Education: 85% (Master's degree)
  • Overall: 75% (good keyword match)
Final Score = (60 × 0.40) + (100 × 0.25) + (85 × 0.20) + (75 × 0.15)
= 24 + 25 + 17 + 11.25
= 77.25% → 77%
77% - Good Match

🏆 Step 4: Ranking (Sorting Candidates)

4 What is Ranking?

After scoring all candidates, we sort them from highest to lowest score to identify the best matches.

Ranking Process:

  1. Calculate final scores for all candidates
  2. Sort descending (highest score first)
  3. Assign grades based on score ranges
  4. Group by quality for easy filtering

Grade System:

Score Range Grade Badge Meaning
85-100 Excellent Excellent Perfect or near-perfect match
70-84 Good Good Strong match, worth interviewing
55-69 Fair Fair Decent match, some gaps
40-54 Below Average Below Avg Weak match, significant gaps
0-39 Poor Poor Not a good fit

Focus on candidates scoring 70% or higher. They have the core skills and experience needed for the role.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I adjust the scoring weights?

A: Yes! You can customize the weights for Skills, Experience, Education, and Overall match. Just make sure they add up to 100%.

Q: What if a resume doesn't have all required skills?

A: The candidate will still be scored, but their Skills score will be lower. They might still rank well if they excel in Experience and Education.

Q: How accurate is the experience detection?

A: Very accurate for standard formats like "5+ years" or "2018-2023". May miss unusual formats or implicit experience.

Q: Can I see the raw scores for each component?

A: Yes! Click on any candidate card to see the detailed breakdown of Skills, Experience, Education, and Overall scores.

Q: What's the difference between "matched" and "missing" skills?

A: Matched skills are those the candidate has that the job requires. Missing skills are required by the job but not found in the resume.

Q: Why use Hybrid mode instead of AI mode?

A: Hybrid mode is faster, cheaper, and more consistent while still providing semantic understanding. AI mode is best when you need very detailed explanations.
